    Operation Briefing: Silent Berth

    OPERATION
    01

    Situation SITUATION

    23:41, Berth 3, Meridian Port. Sea fog has degraded lighting and communications across the port, and dispatch has been out of contact since 22:58. Access-control logs show three unmarked container trucks entering the warehouse district at 23:12 without registration. Dock watch reported an argument and what sounded like a single gunshot before communications cut out.

    Intelligence assesses that a small armed group is operating on site, connected to a batch of impounded cargo manifests. Night-shift workers are still inside the port; at least three are unaccounted for.

    Mission MISSION

    1. Primary · Recover the manifestRecover the hidden cargo manifest from the BAY 3 office.
    2. Primary · Gather dispatch evidenceEnter the port dispatch building and extract surveillance and registration records.
    3. Primary · Protect civiliansMake contact with the night-shift workers and escort them to the south gate rally point.
    4. Secondary · Handle armed suspectsPrefer surrender, then detain (approach and press E). Detention is better than killing.
    5. ExtractionReturn to the south gate armored vehicle once intelligence recovery is complete.

    Rules of Engagement ROE / KEY POINTS

    Observe first, then give the order

    In sea fog you cannot distinguish civilians from gunmen by silhouette. Hold Space for tactical pause, keep the crosshair on the target until identification completes (target-card bar full). Firing before identification counts as a "blind shot" and costs points.

    Three squad orders

    1 Formation follow — the squad advances with you in wedge formation. 2 Hold position — the squad stops and watches its current facing. 3 Move and clear — the squad advances by bounding overwatch to your crosshair point and clears that area.

    Fire discipline

    By default the squad fires only when threatened or ordered to "move and clear"; otherwise it reports first. Hitting a civilian is an immediate mission-failure condition.

    Extraction

    Once the manifest and evidence are recovered, an RTB extraction point appears at the south gate. The whole squad must be present.
