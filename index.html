    Situation

    23:41, Meridian Port, Berth 3. Sea fog has degraded site lighting and comms; dispatch has been silent since 22:58. Gate log shows three unmarked box trucks entered the bays unregistered at 23:12. Quay watch reported raised voices and a probable gunshot, then went off air.

    Assessment: a small armed crew is on site, after a held cargo manifest. Night crew have not been evacuated. At least three unaccounted for.

    Mission

    1. Primary · Recover manifestRetrieve the hidden cargo manifest from the Bay 3 site office.
    2. Primary · Secure dispatch evidenceEnter the dispatch building. Pull CCTV and the gate log.
    3. Primary · Protect civiliansLocate the night crew and escort them to the south gate RV.
    4. Secondary · Armed suspectsCall for surrender, then detain (close in, E). Detained beats dead.
    5. ExfilWith the intel in hand, RTB to the APC at the south gate.

    Rules of engagement

    Observe, then order

    In fog a silhouette will not tell a worker from a gunman. Hold Space for tactical pause and keep the reticle on the contact until ID completes (target card bar full). Rounds fired before ID count as blind shots.

    Three squad orders

    1 Follow — squad moves with you in a wedge. 2 Hold — squad stops and covers its current arc. 3 Move & clear — squad bounds to your reticle point and clears it.

    Fire discipline

    The squad fires only when threatened or under a Move & clear order; otherwise they report first. Harming a civilian is a mission failure condition.

    Exfil

    With manifest and evidence recovered, an RTB point opens at the south gate. The whole squad must be present.
